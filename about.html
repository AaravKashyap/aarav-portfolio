Background

I've spent the last five years in robotics, mostly through VEX Robotics competition — first as a team member, then as team captain, qualifying for state and world championships along the way. Alongside competition robots, I've led a 150+ member robotics club as President.

My main project going into college is a tendon-driven robotic hand I designed and built independently — a from-scratch CAD assembly and electronics build, controllable by keyboard or by mimicking my own hand through a webcam in real time. That's the work I'm most proud of, and the one I want front and center here.
